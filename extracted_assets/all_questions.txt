PACK: HOUDINI | Shem Shelley's Questions from the Gristle
DESC: A provocative set of existential, absurd, and darkly humorous questions designed to unsettle and provoke deep reflection. Written by Shem Shelley for HOUDINI Magazine.
============================================================

1. If you came across the Fountain of Youth, would you piss in it?
2. Imagine everyone you'd ever known was a construct created specifically to alter your true path. How would you react?
3. What would be the cruelest punishment you would devise for yourself?
4. If all actions are reactions, what action have you taken recently that was born from the most petty reaction?
5. Can the blood of a god rejuvenate the ill minds of humankind, or would it take something less fantastical?
6. If we swapped bodies, how many acts of kindness would it take for you to get bored of kindness, knowing it wasn't you receiving the thanks?
7. Do you believe that not believing is itself a form of believing?
8. Will the call of the bird in the dusk of the mind expunge all imaginary disease?
9. The earth is plagued with violent invaders that exist only to devastate all they touch. Do you agree these invaders should be eradicated?
10. How much would you pay someone to always say, "You probably don't need to waste money on this," every time you are about to purchase something?
11. You wouldn't willingly eat poison, so why don't you treat your mind like your stomach and be far more careful in what you consume?
12. If a device was invented that reset all of humankind except you, what would be the first system you set in place to govern them?
13. Which god would you choose to have a fistfight with?
14. Put your music on shuffle and ask, "What sound will I make when I die?"
15. What is the worst sentence you can think of?
16. If you had to destroy all art mediums (painting, film, books, games, etc.) to ensure only one survives, what would you choose?
17. What's your favorite issue of FROM UTOPIA?

PACK: HOUDINI | Late Night Talks With Your Best Friend
DESC: A heart-to-heart set of questions meant for those deep, 2 a.m. conversations with someone who knows you best. Perfect for reminiscing, reflecting, and deepening the bond. Written by [Hap.e] for HOUDINI Magazine.
============================================================

5. What's a memory that you think about fondly?
6. What would you describe as being the peak of our friendship?
7. What would you describe as being the lowest pit of our friendship?
8. In what ways would your life be different if we were not this close?
9. What's a quality, feeling, or emotion in you that often comes about when we're together?
10. How does our friendship differ from your other friendships?
11. What's something we definitely need to carry on doing in our friendship?
12. What's a weakness in our friendship that we should tackle?
13. Do you think we're opposites or the same (in terms of personality, values, morals, etc.)?
14. What are some things you can do with me, but not others?
15. What are some things you can't do with me, but can with others?
16. In a moment of weakness, how would you want me to support you?
17. In a moment of strength, how would you want me to celebrate with you?
18. In a moment of conflict, how would you want me to approach you?

PACK: Questions for small-talk condition
DESC: These questions are used for the small-talk condition in the research paper 'The Experimental Generation of Interpersonal Closeness: A Procedure and Some Preliminary Findings' from 1997 by Arthur Aron, Edward Melinat, Elaine N. Aron, Robert Darrin Vallone and Renee J. Bator. It is recommended by the authors that each participant should answer the questions and share it with each others.
============================================================

1. When was the last time you walked for more than an hour? Describe where you went and what you saw.
2. What was the best gift you ever received and why?
3. If you had to move from your current city where would you go, and what would you miss the most about it?
4. How did you celebrate last Halloween?
5. Do you read a newspaper often and which do you prefer? Why?
6. What is a good number of people to have in a student household and why?
7. If you could invent a new flavor of ice cream, what would it be?
8. What is the best restaurant you've been to in the last month that you partner hasn't been to? Tell your partner about it.
9. Describe the last pet you owned.
10. What is your favorite holiday? Why?
11. Tell your partner the funniest thing that ever happened to you when you were with a small child.
12. What gifts did you receive on your last birthday?
13. Describe the last time you went to the zoo.
14. Tell the names and ages of your family members, including grandparents, aunts and uncles, and where they were born (to the extent you know this information).
15. One of you say a word, the next say a word that starts with the last letter of the word just said. Do this until you have said 50 words. Any words will do - you aren't making a sentence.
16. Do you like to get up early or stay up late? Is there anything funny that has resulted from this?
17. Where are you from? Name all of the places you've lived.
18. What is your favorite class at any school so far? Why?
19. What did you do this summer?
20. What gifts did you receive last Christmas/Hanukkah?
21. Who is your favorite actor of your own gender? Describe a favorite scene in which this person has acted.
22. What was your impression of at any school the first time you ever visited?
23. What is the best TV show you've seen in the last month that your partner hasn't seen? Tell your partner about it.
24. What is your favorite holiday? Why?
25. Where did you go to high school? What was your high school like?
26. What is the best book you've read in the last three month that you partner hasn't read? Tell your partner about it.
27. What foreign country would you most like to visit? What attracts you to this place?
28. Do you prefer digital watches and clocks or the kind with hands? Why?
29. Describe your mother's best friend.
30. What are the advantages and disadvantages of artificial Christmas trees?
31. How often do you get your hair cut? Where do you go? Have you ever had a really bad haircut experience?
32. Did you have a class pet when you were in elementary school? Do you remember the pet's name?
33. Do you think left-handed people are more creative than right-handed people?
34. What is the last concert you saw? How many of that band's albums do you own? Had you seen them before? Where?
35. Do you subscribe to any magazines? Which ones? What have you subscribed to in the past?
36. Were you ever in a school play? What was your role? What was the plot of the play? Did anything funny ever happen when you were on stage?

PACK: 21 questions game
DESC: The 21 Questions Game is basically a way of getting to know someone better. At its core the the game is just asking and answering questions. So if you want to simply ask and answer the questions below, that works. But you can also use these questions for a gamified conversation.
============================================================

1. What do most people think about you that is absolutely not true?
2. What is the TLDR version of your life?
3. Do you think the convenience of technology is worth the loss of privacy that comes with it?
4. Is it okay to sacrifice one life to save ten? If you said yes, would you change answer if the one life was a friend's life and the ten were strangers? If no, why not?
5. What are the consequences of everyone having instant distraction at their fingertips?
6. What should they teach in high school but don't?
7. Which movie or book do you think is ridiculously overrated?
8. Who would you most like to sit next to on a 10 hour flight and why?
9. What are two things you know you should how to do but don't?
10. You have $100 to burn, all your friends are busy and you have the whole day to yourself, what do you do?
11. What makes you say "What was I thinking?" when you look back on your life?
12. How useful would you be in a zombie apocalypse? What skills do you have that would help you and others survive?
13. What is something you've done that you wish you could undo?
14. What inscription do you want on your gravestone?
15. What is something that can't be taught and can only be learned with age?
16. If you could be born again and choose what nationality, gender, and race to be, what would you choose? Why?
17. If you could live your life again knowing what you do now, what would you change?
18. How much do you change when you know no one is around?
19. How much do you know about the world outside your country?
20. What is a cause that is worth dedicating your life to?
21. What should parents stop teaching their children?
